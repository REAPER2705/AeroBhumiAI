CONFIDENCE CALCULATION (REAL, NOT AI ACCURACY)
=============================================================

Deterministic spatial confidence:
- Outside percentage > 20%: +30 pts
- Outside percentage > 5%: +20 pts
- Affected area > 100m²: +15 pts
- Evidence snapshot present: +15 pts
- Spatial encroachment detected: +10 pts
- Base: 50 pts
- Clamped: 0-100

Bands:
- 80-100: HIGH
- 60-79: MEDIUM
- <60: LOW
